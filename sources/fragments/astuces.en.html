Tips

Anti-Sukamon
If your Digimon's dirt gauge fills up completely, it will digivolve into Sukamon, a rather unpleasant companion, it must be said.
To get rid of it, go visit the Sukamon King. He can be found at the dump north of Gear Savanna. He will gladly restore your Digimon to its original appearance.

Easy Money
In Freezeland, on the ice floe, there are three Mojyamon who want to trade with you. The one at the bottom left is particularly interesting as he can help you earn a lot of Money easily.
Start by buying as many "HP Disk M" as possible at 500 Bits each from the capital. Then trade them to the Mojyamon for the same number of "S Defense ROMs". Sell those back for 2,000 Bits each, making a substantial profit.
You can repeat this technique as many times as you like, as long as you haven't traded three different items with that particular Mojyamon.
