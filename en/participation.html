Would you like to contribute to the site's development?

Wonderful! Contact me and share your ideas, suggestions, comments and more :)

If you'd like to help but don't know where to start, here's what I'm currently looking for:

- People who can take screenshots while playing in windowed mode 640x480 with ePSXe, at key moments in the game (see the walkthrough), such as the location where a recruitable Digimon is discovered, places with obstacles to overcome, the curling mini-game, the character while fishing...

- An image of each Digimon Style, more precisely the icons found in the Digimon info screen (fire, fighting, nature, insect, bird, ice, etc...), if you can provide two different ones for each style (since they're animations), that would allow me to create a small .gif for each :)

All contributions will be credited on the site in a future Contributions section :D
