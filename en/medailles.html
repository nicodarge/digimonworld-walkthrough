Medals

Game Medals

In Digimon World, you can earn medals by accomplishing certain very important or seemingly impossible actions.
Here is a list of the conditions required to earn medals

- Catch 100 fish
- Complete the game
- Obtain 999,999 Bits
- Play for 10 Digimon Years
- Obtain one copy of every card in the game
- Win 100 tournaments in Greymon's arena
- Win the Humanoid, Animal, and Dino Cups
- Win the D, C, B, A, and S tournaments
- Recruit all the Digimon in the game
- Raise all the Digimon in the game
- Learn all the attacks in the game
- Obtain a Digimon with all stats at maximum
- Achieve a perfect score of 10 in Curling
- Win all the cups in the Ice, Martial Arts, Air tournaments, etc.
- Win all the Version tournament cups
