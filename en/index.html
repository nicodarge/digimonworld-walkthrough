Welcome

Preservation site. This is a restoration of saetta.free.fr/DigimonWorld, a French fan-site that is now gone (PHP was disabled on Free's personal pages). The content was recovered from the Wayback Machine and reworked. The original forum and guestbook no longer exist and have been removed.

Digimon World (PlayStation, 1999) is a game where you raise a Digimon, a bit like a Tamagotchi, to make it strong enough to help the Digimon of File Island rebuild their town. This guide gathers everything you need to finish it.

Good to know: a bug present in several French releases prevents you from entering the Ogre Fortress (Great Canyon). Whamon and Shellmon then become unrecruitable; you can still beat the game, but it is harder.

Raising your Digimon well

  • Only feed it when it asks: overfeeding, or the wrong food, can make it sick.
  • Take it to the toilet as soon as it asks, otherwise it loses happiness and you lose discipline.
  • Let it sleep when it asks: it then recovers all its HP and MP.
  • Tired? Let it rest for an hour with Punimon or Centarumon in town.
  • Hurt in battle: use a bandage quickly. Sick: a medicine, or Centarumon for 300 Bits.
